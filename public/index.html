Privacy & Data

How We Use Your Data

Transparency is core to our mission. Here's exactly why and how we request access to your data.

Why We Access Your Gmail

Trimly requests read-only access to your Gmail to automatically scan for subscription receipts and invoices. This allows us to identify your active subscriptions, track recurring payments, and alert you before renewals — saving you time and money.

Your Data Stays Private

We never read your personal emails, store email content on our servers, or share your data with third parties. Access is strictly limited to detecting subscription-related receipts. You can revoke access at any time from your Google account settings.

Google API Compliance

Trimly complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your data is handled with the highest security standards.
