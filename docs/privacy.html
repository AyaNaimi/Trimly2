Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Trimly ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

By using Trimly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Financial Data: Transaction records, budget categories, and subscription information that you manually enter or import.
  • Communications: Information you provide when contacting our support team.

2.2 Information Collected via Google API (Gmail)

Important: When you grant Trimly access to your Gmail account, we use the Google API to scan your emails solely to detect and identify subscription-related emails (receipts, invoices, billing notifications). We do NOT:

  • Read the content of your personal emails
  • Store raw email content on our servers
  • Share your email data with any third parties
  • Use your email data for advertising or marketing purposes

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers.
  • Usage Data: App features used, session duration, crash logs.
  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited (for web landing page).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • To process and analyze your financial transactions and subscriptions
  • To detect and categorize your subscription expenses from email receipts
  • To send you notifications about upcoming payments and budget alerts
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Google API Compliance — Limited Use

Trimly's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • Data obtained from Gmail is used only to provide the email scanning feature within the Trimly app.
  • We do not use Gmail data for any purpose other than detecting subscription-related emails.
  • We do not transfer Gmail data to any third party.
  • Humans will not read your Gmail data except as reasonably necessary to provide the feature, fix bugs, or as required by law.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: Your financial data is primarily stored on your device using encrypted local storage.
  • Cloud Sync: If enabled, your data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • No Email Storage: Email content scanned for subscription detection is processed in real-time and is never stored on our servers.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following cases:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party providers (e.g., Supabase, Stripe) that assist in operating our app, subject to strict data protection agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with prior notice.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access & Portability: You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.
  • Revoke Google Access: You can revoke Trimly's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Security Settings or within the Trimly app.
  • Opt-Out: You can disable notifications and email scanning at any time in the app settings.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services. When you delete your account, all your data is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. Email scan results are not retained after processing.

9. Children's Privacy

Trimly is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
